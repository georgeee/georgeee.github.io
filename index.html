WHAT I WORK ON

Small language models

Testing the limits of how far small models can go.

Diane is on-premise, high-quality multilingual dictation, powered by a single 24 GB GPU server. Using Diane for speech recognition, I'm experimenting with a customer-support bot that runs entirely on a fine-tuned Qwen 4B.

Diane →

Agentic coding

Lodge runs AI agents on your own server with first-class isolation and no VMs, managed from Telegram. Bosc, a mixed-reality video game that brings a child's drawing to life on screen, is built with Lodge, and lets players develop their own worlds with a coding agent, without seeing any code.

Lodge →Bosc →

Protocols

Consensus and networking protocols run through most of my career, most notably in my work on Cardano and Mina.

At Yak this continues as Molt Petit, a deliberately small BFT consensus protocol with deterministic finality and a machine-checked safety proof in Lean 4, and Ledger, built on it: financial records that stay correct and private even when an insider, human or AI, has access to every server.

Molt Petit →Ledger →

Formal verification

Formal verification has always had an economics problem: for most software, proofs cost too much to write and maintain.

I think coding agents may change that, while the trusted checker stays small and deterministic. For now this is an interest rather than an active project; the Lean 4 proof behind Molt Petit is where I have tried it so far.
