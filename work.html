2026

Molt Petit · Yak

A research paper, in draft. A BFT consensus protocol built to be small: a fixed roster, a round-robin slot schedule and one density rule in place of votes, quorum certificates and view change. It finalises deterministically and fast, and a light client needs to hold almost nothing.

The depth is in the threat analysis: a rotated key, an extracted key and a key briefly in the wrong hands are three different events, each with a defined path back to safety. The light-client safety results are machine-checked in Lean 4 against the Rust implementation, with much of the proof work done by AI agents. Preprint and artifact.
